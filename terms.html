03

The 30-Day Promise

This is the promise we make on our website, word for word:

Run your AI staff for 30 days. If it hasn't paid for itself by the end of your first month, tell us and we refund that month in full.

Here is exactly how it works.

  • It applies to your first month's service fee only, which is what you pay us to build and run your AI staff.
  • Ask in writing, by email, within 30 days of your go-live date. Go-live is the day your AI staff start working with real customers, not the day you sign up.
  • One claim per business.
  • It does not cover third-party pass-through costs. Telephony numbers, SMS and call charges, and voice minutes are billed by the providers who supply them, and they are not ours to refund.
  • We process the refund within 14 days of agreeing it, back to the payment method you used.
  • No questions and no exit interview. You do not have to justify the decision.

The service is month to month either way. There is no lock-in and no minimum term. Cancel any time by giving us 14 days' written notice, and we wind your staff down at the end of that notice period.

Your AI staff are only as good as what they are given, so a few things sit with you.

05

Service guardrails

These are hard rails in the software, not promises in a brochure. They apply to every business we run staff for.

  • Your AI staff never invent a price. Prices and quotes come only from the pricing rules you give us, word for word. You can switch quoting off entirely.
  • Spend caps. Every worker runs under a spend cap, so a fault cannot run up a bill.
  • Quiet hours. Outbound messages are held outside the hours you set.
  • Human handoff. Anything the AI cannot handle is handed to you, or to whoever you nominate, every time.
  • Supervised warm-up. Every worker starts supervised. You approve the messages before anything goes live in front of a customer.

We monitor the service, and if something goes wrong we tell you rather than waiting for you to find it.
