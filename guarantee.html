Guarantee

The 60-day money-back guarantee

We want you to judge Applied Intelligence on results, not on a sales call. So here is the whole guarantee, in full, with nothing hidden in a footnote.

Last updated: 16 September 2026

On this page

  2. What is not covered

  5. How to claim

This is the promise we make on our website and in our advertising, word for word:

Run your AI staff for 60 days. If they aren't earning their keep, tell us and we refund your service fees for that period.

01

What is covered

The monthly service fee for your first 60 days of live operation. That is what you pay us to run your AI staff. If you ask for a refund inside that window, we refund those service fees in full.

02

What is not covered

  • The one-off setup and build fee. That is the work of configuring, training and testing your AI staff, and it is finished before you go live. The build is yours to keep.
  • Third-party pass-through costs. Telephony numbers, SMS and call charges, and voice minutes are billed by the providers who supply them, and they are not ours to refund.
03

When the clock starts

On your go-live date, which is the day your AI staff first take a live call or reply to a live enquiry. Not the day you sign up. Onboarding time never eats into your window.

04

The one condition

Your calls and enquiries have to actually reach your AI staff for the period. If the number was never forwarded, or the integration was switched off, there is nothing for us to be judged on.

That is the only condition. There is no minimum usage, no scorecard to hit, and nothing you have to prove.

05

How to claim

  • Email nickjmatthews@pm.me with "refund" in the subject line, any time inside the 60 days.
  • We do not ask you to justify it. No questions and no exit interview.
  • We process the refund within 10 business days of agreeing it, back to the payment method you used.
  • We will ask for fifteen minutes of your time to learn what did not work. That is a request, not a condition, and saying no changes nothing.
  • One claim per business. The guarantee applies to your first 60 days with us.
06

After a refund

Your AI staff stop. Your number goes back to whatever you had before. You keep every record and transcript from the period, and we delete your data on request.

The service is month to month either way. There is no lock-in and no minimum term. Cancel any time by giving us 14 days' written notice, and we wind your staff down at the end of that notice period.

07

Your consumer rights

Applied Intelligent Systems & Automations, trading as Applied Intelligence. This guarantee sits on top of your rights under Australian Consumer Law, which we cannot and do not try to limit. Nothing on this page reduces a right you already have.

The full terms of the service are in our terms of service, and how we handle your information is set out in our privacy policy.
